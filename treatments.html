Classical Purification

Panchakarma: the five actions

Panchakarma is Ayurveda's deepest cleansing programme. Preparatory oil and heat therapies (snehana and swedana) loosen toxins, which are then eliminated through one or more of the five classical procedures. The programme usually lasts 7 to 21 days.

Vamana (Therapeutic Emesis)

Controlled, medicated emesis that clears excess Kapha from the stomach and chest.

Helpful for: asthma, allergies, skin disorders, obesity

Virechana (Purgation)

Herbal purgation that removes excess Pitta and toxins from the liver, gall bladder and intestines.

Helpful for: skin diseases, digestive disorders, acidity, diabetes

Vasti (Medicated Enema)

Herbal decoction or oil enemas, considered the most important Panchakarma therapy for Vata disorders.

Helpful for: back pain, arthritis, paralysis, constipation, neurological conditions

Nasya (Nasal Therapy)

Medicated oils or powders administered through the nose to cleanse the head and neck region.

Helpful for: sinusitis, migraine, cervical spondylosis, hair and scalp problems

Raktamokshana (Blood Purification)

Classical blood-letting techniques such as leech therapy to remove impurities from the blood.

Helpful for: chronic skin diseases, varicose veins, localised inflammation

Kerala Special Therapies

External therapies unique to Kerala

Kerala's physicians developed a family of oil, poultice and pouring therapies that nourish the tissues, relieve pain and deeply relax the nervous system. They are used on their own or as part of a Panchakarma programme.

Abhyanga

Full-body massage with warm medicated oil, usually followed by a herbal steam bath.

Helpful for: fatigue, stiffness, poor circulation, stress, general rejuvenation

Shirodhara

A continuous stream of warm oil, buttermilk or decoction poured over the forehead.

Helpful for: insomnia, anxiety, migraine, hypertension, nervous disorders

Pizhichil

Warm medicated oil is rhythmically poured over the body while being gently massaged.

Helpful for: rheumatic conditions, paralysis, muscular weakness, rejuvenation

Njavarakizhi

Poultices of Njavara rice cooked in milk and herbal decoction applied over the body.

Helpful for: arthritis, muscle wasting, neurological weakness, joint pain

Elakizhi

Poultices of fresh medicinal leaves fried in oil applied with gentle massage.

Helpful for: joint pain, sports injuries, spondylosis, sciatica

Kati Vasti

Warm medicated oil retained over the lower back inside a dough ring.

Helpful for: low back pain, disc problems, sciatica, lumbar spondylosis

Janu Vasti

Warm medicated oil retained over the knee joint.

Helpful for: knee osteoarthritis, knee pain and stiffness

Greeva Vasti

Warm medicated oil retained over the neck region.

Helpful for: cervical spondylosis, neck pain, stiffness, frozen shoulder

Sirovasti

Warm medicated oil held on the head inside a leather cap for a fixed time.

Helpful for: facial paralysis, insomnia, migraine, neurological disorders

Thalapothichil

A cooling herbal paste applied over the scalp and covered with leaves.

Helpful for: insomnia, stress, hair fall, scalp conditions

Udvartana

Vigorous massage with herbal powders that tones the skin and body.

Helpful for: obesity, cellulite, poor circulation, skin dullness

Nasya & Dhoomapana

Nasal therapy and herbal smoke inhalation for the head and respiratory tract.

Helpful for: sinusitis, allergic rhinitis, headache, hoarseness

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How long does a Panchakarma programme take?

A full programme usually takes 7 to 21 days depending on your condition and constitution. Shorter wellness and pain-relief packages of 3 to 7 days are also available. The physician decides the duration after consultation.

Do I need to stay at the hospital?

Not always. Many therapies can be taken as an out-patient during OP hours. Intensive Panchakarma and rehabilitation programmes give the best results with a residential stay, because rest, diet and routine are part of the treatment. Our in-patient facility offers ward, single, double and air-conditioned rooms with WiFi, and meals come from our yoga and diet-regulated Ayurvedic kitchen.

Are the treatments safe alongside my current medicines?

Yes, in most cases. Please bring your medical reports and a list of current medicines to the consultation so the physician can plan treatment safely.

What should I bring for an in-patient stay?

Comfortable cotton clothing, personal toiletries, your medical records and any regular medicines. Everything needed for the therapies is provided by the hospital.

Who will treat me?

All treatment at LAHPC is planned and supervised by our chief physician, Dr. Litty C.J., who holds a B.A.M.S. and an M.D. (Ayu) in Panchakarma. Therapies are carried out by professionally qualified paramedical staff and trained therapists.

Out-patient consultation is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM. In-patient care is available round the clock. Please call ahead on +91 9037 555 036 to fix a time.
