Why Choose LAHPC

One Health. One Future.

True health is harmony between body, mind and environment. Newly inaugurated in September 2026 and ISO 9001:2026 certified, here is what makes Lissy Ayurveda Hospital a place people trust with their wellbeing.

Home/Why LAHPC
Dr. Litty C.J.Chief Physician · BAMS, MD (Ayu)
01

Personalised treatment, every time

No two patients are the same. Every plan at LAHPC starts with a detailed consultation, pulse diagnosis and a review of your history and reports. Therapies, medicines, diet and duration are all tailored to you.

02

Authentic Kerala Ayurveda

We follow the classical texts and Kerala's living tradition of Panchakarma. Treatments are performed the traditional way, without shortcuts, using genuine herbal medicines and medicated oils.

03

A Panchakarma specialist and trained staff

Our chief physician, Dr. Litty C.J., BAMS, MD (Ayu) Panchakarma, supervises every treatment. Professionally qualified paramedical staff and trained therapists carry out the procedures with care, hygiene and respect for your comfort and privacy.

04

Serene, green surroundings

Our newly built hospital in green, peaceful Mattathur is calm and away from city noise. With a 15-bed in-patient facility offering ward, single, double and air-conditioned rooms with WiFi, it is the ideal setting for residential programmes where rest is part of the medicine.

05

Complete care under one roof

Daily OP consultation, Panchakarma and Kerala therapies, a 15-bed in-patient unit, an Ayurvedic pharmacy and a yoga and diet-regulated Ayurvedic kitchen are all under one roof, so your treatment is seamless from first visit to follow-up.

06

Honest guidance that lasts

We tell you clearly what Ayurveda can and cannot do for your condition, and we send you home with diet and lifestyle advice so the benefits continue long after treatment.

Our Commitment to You

Wellness of Body & Mind

The words on our logo are a promise. Every decision at LAHPC, from the medicines we stock to the way our rooms are kept, is guided by these commitments.

  • Safety first. Every therapy is prescribed and supervised by a qualified physician.
  • Hygiene always. Therapy rooms, linen and equipment are cleaned to strict standards.
  • Genuine medicines. Only authentic herbal formulations and medicated oils are used.
  • Respect and privacy. Your comfort and dignity protected at all times.
  • Food as medicine. Meals from our yoga and diet-regulated Ayurvedic kitchen support every treatment.
  • Transparent advice. Clear information on treatment, duration and expected results.
  • Certified quality. Our quality management system is ISO 9001:2026 certified and the hospital is registered with Mattathur Grama Panchayat.
