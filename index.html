About LAHPC

Timeless Wisdom for a healthier Tomorrow

Lissy Ayurveda Hospital & Panchakarma Centre (LAHPC) is an Ayurvedic hospital located at Ithupadam, Mattathur, near Kodakara in Thrissur district, Kerala, the heartland of Ayurveda.

We practise Ayurveda the way it was meant to be practised: through careful diagnosis of your constitution (prakriti), understanding the root cause of imbalance, and treating it with authentic Panchakarma therapies, herbal medicines, diet and lifestyle guidance.

  • Classical Panchakarma detoxification & rejuvenation programmes
  • Consultation, out-patient treatments and in-patient residential care
  • Genuine herbal medicines and medicated oils
  • Personalised diet, yoga and lifestyle guidance

Our Treatments

Panchakarma & Ayurvedic Therapies

From deep detoxification to gentle rejuvenation, every therapy is prescribed after a detailed consultation and performed by trained therapists under physician supervision.

🫗

Panchakarma

The five classical purification procedures — Vamana, Virechana, Vasti, Nasya and Raktamokshana — to cleanse the body of accumulated toxins and restore doshic balance.

💧

Shirodhara

A continuous stream of warm medicated oil poured over the forehead. Deeply calming for stress, anxiety, insomnia, migraine and nervous disorders.

🌿

Abhyanga

Full-body therapeutic oil massage that nourishes the tissues, improves circulation, relieves muscle fatigue and promotes deep relaxation.

🌾

Njavarakizhi & Elakizhi

Herbal poultice therapies using medicated rice or fresh leaves, effective for joint pain, arthritis, muscular stiffness and neurological weakness.

🛁

Pizhichil

The "royal treatment": warm medicated oil is rhythmically poured over the body with gentle massage. Excellent for rheumatic conditions and rejuvenation.

🦴

Kati Vasti & Janu Vasti

Warm oil retained over the lower back or knee joint to relieve back pain, disc problems, sciatica and knee osteoarthritis.

👃

Nasya

Medicated nasal therapy for sinusitis, allergic rhinitis, headache, cervical spondylosis and disorders of the head and neck.

✨

Udvartana

Herbal powder massage that tones the skin, reduces excess fat and cellulite, and improves metabolism and lymphatic flow.

🧘

Rasayana & Wellness

Rejuvenation programmes combining therapies, herbal medicines, diet and yoga to strengthen immunity and slow the ageing process.
