About LAHPC

Timeless Wisdom for a healthier Tomorrow

Lissy Ayurveda Hospital & Panchakarma Centre (LAHPC) is an Ayurvedic hospital located at Ithupadam, Mattathur, near Kodakara in Thrissur district, Kerala, the heartland of Ayurveda. The hospital was inaugurated on 24 September 2026 by Sri. Ramesh Chennithala, Minister for Home Affairs, Government of Kerala, and is led by Chief Physician Dr. Litty C.J.

We practise Ayurveda the way it was meant to be practised: through careful diagnosis of your constitution (prakriti), understanding the root cause of imbalance, and treating it with authentic Panchakarma therapies, herbal medicines, diet and lifestyle guidance.

  • Classical Panchakarma under a qualified Panchakarma specialist
  • Daily OP consultation and a 15-bed in-patient facility
  • Ayurvedic pharmacy with genuine medicines and medicated oils
  • Yoga and diet-regulated Ayurvedic kitchen

A Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery with a postgraduate degree in Panchakarma, she begins each case with a detailed consultation to understand your constitution and the root cause of your condition before prescribing a personalised programme of therapies, medicines and diet.

Under her care the hospital offers classical Panchakarma, treatment of Vata and joint disorders, women's health and pre- and post-natal care, Ayurvedic eye and scalp care, lifestyle disease management and rehabilitation after paralysis.

Our Treatments

Panchakarma & Ayurvedic Therapies

From deep detoxification to gentle rejuvenation, every therapy is prescribed after a detailed consultation and performed by trained therapists under the supervision of our Panchakarma specialist, Dr. Litty C.J.

🫗

Panchakarma

Vamana, Virechana, Vasti, Nasya and Raktamokshana: the five classical purification procedures that remove accumulated toxins and restore doshic balance.

🦴

Vata & Joint Disorders

Osteoarthritis (Sandhivata), rheumatoid arthritis (Amavata), gout (Vatarakta), back pain and spondylosis treated with Vasti, Kizhi and Pizhichil.

🤱

Pre- & Post-natal Care

Safe care through pregnancy and traditional post-delivery packages that restore the mother's strength, health and figure.

🌸

Women's Health

Menstrual problems, infertility, leucorrhoea, menopause and PCOS managed with medicines, diet and therapies.

👁️

Eye Care (Netra Chikitsa)

Cataract, glaucoma, dry eye, night blindness, refractive errors and diabetic retinopathy with classical Ayurvedic eye therapies.

💆

Head & Scalp (Shiro Roga)

Dandruff, hair fall, insomnia and migraine relieved with Shirodhara, Thalapothichil and Nasya.

⚖️

Lifestyle Disease Management

Diabetes, obesity, blood pressure, cholesterol and hormonal imbalance controlled through medicines, diet and daily routine.

⚡

Paralysis Rehabilitation

Structured in-patient rehabilitation after stroke and paralysis with Abhyanga, Pizhichil, Njavarakizhi and Vasti.

🍃

Digestive Disorders

Acidity, indigestion, constipation and other gastric complaints treated at the root with medicines and diet correction.

🌿

Skin Disorders

Psoriasis, eczema, allergies and chronic skin conditions through purification therapies and herbal medicines.

🫁

Respiratory Disorders

Asthma, sinusitis, allergic rhinitis and recurrent cough managed with Nasya, Dhoomapana and medicines.

🧘

Rejuvenation Packages

Sukha Chikitsa programmes with relaxing therapies, herbal tonics, yoga and Ayurvedic diet to restore vitality.
